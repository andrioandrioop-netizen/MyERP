Paste from Excel

One line per subject — takes 30 seconds for all your courses.

Format

Each line: Course | Subject | Teacher | Sessions/week | Slots

Separators can be Tab (best — copy straight from Excel), |, or ,. The last two columns are optional. If your subject looks like BBADC101-IS, it's auto-split into subject BBADC101 + teacher IS.

How to use — 4 easy steps

No jargon. No setup. Just a clean timetable.

Step 1 · Pick your working days

Click Mon–Sat (or whichever days you work) in the left panel.

Step 2 · Set your time slots

The default is 10–11, 11–12, 12–1, lunch 1–2, 2–3, 3–4, 4–5. Rename them, add more, or mark any as Recess (lunch, library, PET).

Step 3 · Add your courses

Click the big 📋 Paste from Excel button. Copy your course rows straight from Excel and paste — that's it. Every teacher is auto-created.

Step 4 · Hit Generate

Click ⚡ Generate Timetable. Your timetable appears in the "Timetable" tab — one clean grid per course — and a personal weekly view for every teacher in the "Teacher View" tab. Click ⬇ Excel to download everything.

Good to know

• Teachers shared across courses are never double-booked — the engine checks the real wall-clock slot, not just the period index.
• Your work is auto-saved on this computer. Close and reopen safely.
• Use 📤 Save to export a JSON file, and 📥 Import to load it on another PC.
